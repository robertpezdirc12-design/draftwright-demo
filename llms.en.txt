# Tehnična risba PRO

> Tehnična risba PRO (DraftWright) turns a photo of a fence, balcony or terrace into a technical drawing with dimensions, bill of materials and assembly notes for WoodCore WPC systems. It covers transverse and vertical fences, façades, terraces, partition walls and eaves; computes post and batten spacing by Roksal rules and exports PDF, SVG, DXF, STEP and CSV. Runs as a PWA, offline on site.

- Type: web application (PWA) — works offline on site
- License: MIT
- Platforms: modern browsers: Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, iOS
- Exports: PDF, SVG, DXF, STEP; BOM, assembly plan, cutting plan, quotation (CSV); order, checklist, material sheet, Roksal enquiry (TXT)
- Latest version: 2026.11.38 (2026-11-26)
- Material catalogue: WoodCore WPC (Roksal d.o.o.) — profiles, palettes, spacing and fastening rules
- Privacy: no external network calls — photos and measurements stay on the device

## From a photo to a technical WPC fence drawing

Five steps in Tehnična risba PRO: load a photo, calibrate, set the WoodCore system parameters, generate the drawing and export the documentation.

1. **Load a photo** — Use the File menu to load a photo of a fence, balcony or terrace, or open a prepared sample drawing.
2. **Calibrate the image** — Calibrate the image with 2 or 4 points against a known dimension (e.g. post height); the scale is converted to millimetres.
3. **Set the parameters** — Enter length L, height H, board count, the WoodCore profile and the use case (transverse or vertical fence, façade, terrace, partition, eaves).
4. **Generate the drawing** — The app computes post and batten spacing by Roksal rules, adds assembly notes and a checklist, and generates the technical drawing.
5. **Export the documentation** — Export PDF, SVG, DXF or STEP, a bill of materials (CSV), assembly plan, cutting plan, quotation or a Roksal enquiry (TXT).

## WoodCore WPC profiles

### ROMB-67

- Mere: 67 × 26 × 5800 mm (do 145 cm).
- Pritrditev: skrito — L-kotnik levo in desno na stebru, alu cev v sredini profila (montaža brez alu cevi ni mogoča).
- Uporabe: prečna ograja, pokončna ograja, fasada, pregradna stena.
- Omejitve: prečna ograja — razmak med stebri ≤ 1450 mm; pokončna ograja — razmak med stebri ≤ 1450 mm; fasada — razmak podkonstrukcije ≤ 800 mm; pregradna stena — razmak stebrov (pokončno) ≤ 1450 mm.
- Pokončno: vodoravne cevi z razmakom ≤ 1100 mm (vsaj 2 vrsti; število = ⌈H/1100⌉+1).
- Fasada: podkonstrukcija ≤ 800 mm, skupna debelina na izolaciji 71–75 mm.
- Vrtanje: 3.9 vijak → Ø3.2 v alu, Ø4.2–4.5 v WPC; obvezna alu cev v sredini romba.
- Barvne palete: Amazon wood, Ash wood, Golden teak, Burma teak, Oak wood, Rustic oak, Rustic walnut.
- Pravila aplikacije (opomba za risbo):
  - prečna ograja: ROMB-67: razmak med stebri ≤ 1450 mm; predvrtanje +1 mm, vijak 30 mm od roba reza. Pri >1450 mm dodaj vmesno povezavo do <1800 mm, nad tem nov steber.
  - pokončna ograja: ROMB-67: stebri ≤ 1450 mm; vodoravne cevi 3×, razmak ≤ 1100 mm. Vijak 30–150 mm od roba reza. Romb: čepi na koncih; obvezna alu cev v sredini profila — montaža brez nje ni mogoča.
  - fasada: ROMB-67: podkonstrukcija ≤ 800 mm; skupna debelina na izolaciji 71–75 mm; okoli odprtin alu pločevina 2 mm.
  - pregradna stena: ROMB-67: prečno stebri ≤ 1450 mm (odvisno od profila), pokončno 150–180 cm po profilu (57/32 in romb 150; polna 100/128 180 do višine 150 cm, višje 150). Romb: kotnik na vsakem nosilcu levo in desno (145 cm velja samo ob obojestranskih kotnikih); pri >145 cm vmesna povezava iz zgornje cevi (ni v tla) do <180 cm, sicer nov stebriček; pokončno: vodoravne cevi ≤ 110 cm s kotnikom, vertikalni nosilci ≤ 100 cm (kotnik ali ploščat aluminij na vsakem nosilcu); odprtine zakriti (vdor vode do alu cevi); čepi ne garantirajo pred vdorom vode.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which WoodCore WPC profiles does Tehnična risba PRO support?

The app knows six WoodCore profiles: ROMB-67 (67 × 26 × 5800 mm), POLNA-128 (128 × 16.5 × 2200 mm), DESKA-150 (150 × 25 × 4000 mm), POLNA-57/32 (57 × 32 × 5800 mm), POLNA-100 (100 × 12 × 5800 mm) and KUBO-80/42 (80 × 42 × 5800 mm). For each profile it applies the catalogue post and batten spacing and the fastening rules.

### Are the calculations and drawings binding documentation?

No. The calculations are orientational and are not contractual documents; all dimensions are in millimetres (mm) and must be verified on site before fabrication. The quotation is orientational and is not an offer — the contract form governs binding offers and VAT.

### What is the maximum post spacing for a vertical fence?

The maximum post spacing depends on the profile: ROMB-67 up to 1450 mm, POLNA-57/32 up to 1500 mm, and POLNA-100 and POLNA-128 up to 1800 mm for fence heights up to 1500 mm (1500 mm above that). For partition walls the post spacing is 150–180 cm depending on the profile.

### Is an aluminium tube required for the ROMB-67 profile?

Yes. ROMB-67 is mounted hidden with L-brackets left and right on each post; the aluminium tube in the middle of the profile is mandatory and included in the price of the ROMB board — mounting without the aluminium tube is not possible. A 3.9 mm screw uses a Ø3.2 mm hole in the aluminium tube and the ROMB board, and Ø4.2–4.5 mm in the WPC.

### How are WPC boards fastened to the substructure?

The minimum screw offset from the cut edge is 3 cm, up to 15 cm for slab-mounted substructure and 10 cm for side-mounted slabs. The WPC hole is 1 mm larger than the screw (pre-drilling +1 mm); for POLNA-57/32 the WPC hole is smaller than the screw and the substructure hole larger. Screws sit 30–150 mm from the board edge; mounting is done above +5 °C.

### How do I maintain WPC garden furniture?

Clean the surface with water. For extra water-repellent protection apply WEPOS WPC impregnation to a well-cleaned and completely dry surface, especially tabletops and seats.

### How should WPC boards be stored?

Store boards in a dry, ventilated room on a flat, clean support along their full length and width. With improper storage, warranty claims are not accepted.

### What is the warranty for WoodCore WPC?

The warranty is 15 years on the material and 2 years on work performed by Roksal. The shade of the board lightens by 5–10 % in the first year.
